This project was a challenging but very valuable learning experience for me. I did not take Game Development course in the previous semester, so Unity was completely new to me. At the beginning, I struggled a lot with understanding even the basic structure of a Unity project, such as how scripts, assets, prefabs, and scenes are organized and how they work together. Because of that, the learning curve felt quite steep, and at times it was overwhelming.
Fortunately, I was working in a group with two other students who had already taken Game Development and were much more experienced with Unity. They helped me a lot throughout the project, especially during workshops, and I am very grateful for their support and patience. Thanks to them, I was able to slowly understand how Unity works and gain more confidence when working in the editor.
Since I was less experienced with Unity, my contributions during the workshops were often focused on helping with logic and discussing how certain interactions or scripts for 3D objects should work, rather than fully implementing complex scripts on my own. Individually, I mainly worked on building the scenery and structure of the projects. This included adding and organizing assets, creating prefabs, setting up mesh colliders, and preparing the environments so that the we could integrate interactions and gameplay logic later.
One of my contributions was implementing the movement logic for the planets in the AR project. I worked on making the planets orbit with different speeds and around correct axes, which helped reinforce the educational aspect of the solar system. Writing this logic helped me better understand transformations, rotations, and how scripts affect 3D objects over time. Even though the scripts were relatively simple compared to other parts of the project, they were an important step for me in learning how to code in Unity.
In the VR project, I contributed to the interaction logic between objects, especially how trash interacts with different trashcans. I was also involved in setting up environments and testing interactions to make sure everything worked as intended. Testing the builds on actual devices, such as mobile phones for AR and the Meta Quest for VR, was especially useful because it showed how different the experience can be compared to testing only in the Unity editor.
Overall, even though I started with very little Unity experience, I feel that I learned a lot during this project. It helped me understand the basics of Unity, AR and XR projects. If I had more time, I would like to improve my scripting skills and take on more complex programming tasks in future projects. Despite the challenges, I see this project as a strong learning experience that helped me build a solid foundation in Unity and AR/VR development.
